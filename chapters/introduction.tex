\section{Why post-quantum cryptography matters}
Quantum computers threaten many classical assumptions used in public-key cryptography. In particular, Shor's algorithm can efficiently break RSA, Diffie-Hellman, and elliptic-curve systems when a sufficiently large fault-tolerant quantum computer becomes available.
